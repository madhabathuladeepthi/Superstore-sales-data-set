# Superstore-sales-data-set
Sales Overview Dashboard
Context

We are analyzing the Superstore Sales performance across regions, product categories, and time.
The dashboard highlights our key business metrics — Total Sales, Total Orders, Total Customers, and Total Products — and breaks down performance by region, category, and order date.

Middle → Insight

1️⃣ Sales Performance Overview
Total Sales show a steady growth pattern over multiple months.
Seasonal spikes appear during year-end months (Nov–Dec), indicating strong holiday demand.

2️⃣ Regional Insights
Certain regions contribute significantly more to revenue.
The West and East territories commonly lead in both order volume and total sales.
Lower-performing regions may have fewer customers or lower product penetration.

3️⃣ Category Performance
Categories such as Technology or Office Supplies generate the highest sales.
Sub-categories may show variations: Some products sell frequently but with low margins, others have high value but low volume.

4️⃣ Time Trend
The line chart (Order Date → Sales) highlights:
Strong upward trends in multiple months.
Some dips that may correlate with supply issues, pricing, or seasonal effects.

Customer & Shipping Insights
Context

This page focuses on understanding customer behavior, purchase segments, and shipping preferences.
Filters (slicers) such as Region, State, Segment, Order Date, and Category allow deeper exploration.

Middle → Insight

1️⃣ Segment Insights
Certain segments—Consumer or Corporate—drive the most sales.
The total sales by segment clearly show where the business has the strongest presence.

2️⃣ Shipping Behavior
Standard Class usually has the highest number of orders.
Faster shipping modes such as Second Day or Same Day have fewer orders but may represent urgent or high-value purchases.

3️⃣ Interaction of Segment & Shipping
Some segments prefer cheaper shipping (Consumer), while others (Corporate) may choose faster modes for urgency.

4️⃣ Order Table Insights
Using the table (Order ID, Order Date, Customer Name, Product Name, Category, Sales):
We can identify high-value customers.

We can check which products are purchased together.

We can analyze order patterns for seasonality or return likelihood.
